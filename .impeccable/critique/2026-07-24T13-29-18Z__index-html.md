The interface has transformed from a generic, overstimulated AI template into a calm, confident brand presence. The deep terracotta anchors the design, and the removal of the preloader, marquee, and custom cursor makes the experience feel much more professional and accessible. The biggest opportunity now is refining the typography.

- **Committed Brand Colors**: The terracotta palette gives the site a distinct, premium identity.
