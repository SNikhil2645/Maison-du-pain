# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Local customers looking to order custom celebration cakes or view the daily menu.

## Product Purpose
Driving custom cake inquiries and custom order form submissions while showcasing the artisan bakery menu.

## Brand Personality
Warm, artisanal, and elegant.

## Anti-references
Not a generic template or overly tech-focused. It should feel handcrafted and authentic, avoiding sterile or mass-market aesthetics.

## Design Principles
- **Artisanal warmth:** Use materials, colors, and typography that evoke a physical bakery environment.
- **Showcase the craft:** Highlight the high-quality ingredients and the visual appeal of the baked goods.
- **Effortless inquiry:** Ensure the custom cake ordering and inquiry process is seamless and intuitive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard WCAG AA compliance, focusing on legible contrast and full keyboard navigation.
